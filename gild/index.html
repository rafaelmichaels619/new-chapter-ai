FAQ

Before you join the list.

When can I download Gild?

Gild is not on the App Store or Google Play yet. Join the waitlist and New Chapter AI Studio will write when it is available.

Who is Gild for?

Gild is a luxury travel planning app by New Chapter AI Studio for affluent leisure travelers who take five-star trips and want a clear view of what they can splurge on.

Does Gild have hotel partnerships?

No. Gild does not claim a partnership with any hotel or airline. Curated hotels and getaway offers, including stay-longer offers, resort credits, and suite packages, are there to consider. Gild is not the hotel.

What is a Getaway Deal?

A Getaway Deal is a stay-longer offer, a resort credit, or a suite package from a property. The property sets the offer. Gild gathers ones that fit a luxury trip.

How do trip budgets work?

You set a trip budget in your own currency, with a limit for parts of the trip such as the stay, dining, and experiences. Gild shows where there is still room to splurge. It is a planning view, not a limit from your bank.

What is the points wallet?

You can link hotel and airline loyalty programs. Gild shows an estimated cash value of those points beside the rest of the plan. The estimate is for planning. It is not a redemption quote from the program.

Does Gild store my card number?

No. Payments use Apple Pay or Google Pay. Gild does not store card numbers. Apple or Google processes the payment under that company's terms.

Is there a membership, and what does it cost?

Gild has a membership tier. What it includes, and what it costs, is shown in the app before you join. This website does not list a price.

Do you use affiliate links?

Yes. Some hotel, tour, experience, and getaway offers are affiliate links. If you book through one, New Chapter AI Studio may earn a commission from the partner, which may include an experience marketplace such as Viator. The commission is paid by the partner. The privacy policy has the full disclosure.

What is still on the way?

Travel insurance, car rentals, a live currency converter, cost-of-living comparisons, restaurant picks held to a 4.3 rating and above, and a secure travel-document vault that stays on your device. Those are not in the app yet.
