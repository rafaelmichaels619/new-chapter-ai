Privacy Policy

Effective November 21, 2026

Gild is a luxury travel planning app by New Chapter AI Studio for affluent leisure travelers. New Chapter AI Studio (“we”) operates the Gild app and these Gild pages. This policy explains what information those services handle, how payments work, and how affiliate links work.

Affiliate links. Some hotel, tour, experience, and getaway offers in Gild are affiliate links. If you book through an affiliate link, the partner may pay New Chapter AI Studio a commission. Partners can include experience marketplaces such as Viator. The commission is paid by the partner. Gild does not add its own charge for the use of an affiliate link. The full disclosure is in Affiliate links below.

Who we are

The operator is New Chapter AI Studio. Gild is the studio’s luxury travel planning app for people who take five-star leisure trips and want a clear view of what they can splurge on. These pages introduce the app. The app is not yet listed on the App Store or Google Play.

Contact: hello@newchapteraistudio.com.

Where this policy applies

This policy covers the Gild mobile app and the Gild pages published by New Chapter AI Studio, including this page. It is written for people in the United States. If these pages later move to another domain, this policy still covers Gild, and we will update the address on this page.

A hotel, airline, experience marketplace, or app store has its own policy. When you leave Gild for one of those services, that policy applies to what you do there.

Information you give us

We receive what you choose to send.

  • If you join the waitlist by email, we receive your email address, the subject line, and anything else you write. We use that to tell you when Gild is available. A waitlist email is not an account.
  • If you set up Gild, you may tell us a travel style, favorite brands, and dream destinations. We use that to shape the plan.
  • Trip notes you enter, such as dates, a destination, and category limits for a trip budget, are used to show the plan in your currency.
  • If you link a hotel or airline loyalty program, we receive the information needed to show an estimated cash value of those points for planning. Linking is optional.

Please do not email us passport numbers, government identity numbers, or payment card numbers. We do not ask for them by email.

On-device information

A secure travel-document vault is planned and is not available yet. It is designed so travel documents stay on your device and are not uploaded to New Chapter AI Studio for storage. Until that feature is released, do not rely on Gild to hold passports or other travel documents.

Category limits, loyalty estimates, and other planning figures are there to help you decide a trip. They are not a copy of your bank, card, or loyalty account.

Payments

Payments in Gild, including a membership if you join one, use Apple Pay or Google Pay. Gild does not collect card numbers and does not store card numbers. Apple or Google processes the payment under that company’s terms. We may receive a record that a purchase succeeded, such as a product name and a transaction reference, so we can provide what you bought. That record is not your card number.

Affiliate links

Gild is a luxury travel planning app by New Chapter AI Studio for affluent leisure travelers. It helps you plan a trip. It is not a hotel brand, and it is not an experience marketplace.

Some links to hotels, tours, experiences, and getaway offers are affiliate links. If you book through an affiliate link, New Chapter AI Studio may earn a commission from the affiliate partner. The commission is paid by the partner. It is not a separate charge added by Gild for using the link.

Those partners may include hotels, tour operators, and experience marketplaces such as Viator. An affiliate relationship is not an endorsement of Gild by that company. It is not an official partnership. Gild is not an agent of Viator, and Gild is not an agent of, or affiliated with, any hotel brand named on the Gild pages, including Ritz-Carlton, St. Regis, Four Seasons, Aman, and Rosewood. Those names describe the kind of stay an editorial pick may point to. This site does not display their logos.

These marketing pages do not yet send you through to a booking. When the app offers a bookable affiliate link, that offer will be identifiable in the app. The provider sets the price, the availability, the credit, and the package. Confirm those details with the provider before you book.

If you follow an affiliate link, the other site may collect information under its own privacy policy, including through its own cookies. We do not control that collection.

Loyalty programs

Hotel and airline programs are run by those companies. If you link one, Gild uses the connection to show an estimated cash value of your points next to a plan. The estimate is a planning aid. It is not a quote from the airline or hotel, and it is not a promise of what a redemption will be worth. The program’s own terms apply. You can choose not to link a program.

How we use information

We use information to:

  • reply to a waitlist email and tell you when the app is available;
  • build and show your trip plan, including budgets in your currency and Gild picks;
  • show estimated point values for programs you link;
  • provide a membership you choose to buy;
  • keep the app and these pages reliable and secure;
  • meet a legal duty.

We do not sell personal information. We do not share it for other companies’ advertising.

Service providers

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect standard technical data, such as an IP address and browser details, under GitHub’s own policies. Type is loaded from Google Fonts. Google may receive a request for those font files under its own policies.

These pages do not set their own cookies. They do not run advertising trackers or our own analytics cookies.

Apple and Google provide the app stores, Apple Pay, and Google Pay. Their terms and privacy policies cover the store account and the payment.

How long we keep information

We keep a waitlist email for as long as we need it to tell you about availability, unless you ask us to delete it sooner. Planning details you store in the app stay there while you keep them, and are removed when you delete them or when we delete them at your request, except where we must keep a record of a purchase or a legal duty.

Your choices

You may email hello@newchapteraistudio.com to ask what personal information we have from you, to correct it, or to ask us to delete it. If you are a California resident, you may ask us to disclose the categories of personal information we have collected about you and to delete that information, subject to records we are allowed or required to keep.

You can stop using the app at any time. Leaving the waitlist is an email to the same address.

Children

Gild and these pages are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

United States

Gild is directed to travelers in the United States. Planning tools that show a currency use the currency you choose. We do not claim that every hotel, tour, or experience is available in every state.

Changes

We may update this policy. When we do, we will change the effective date at the top. If a change materially alters how we handle information the app already has, we will provide any notice the law requires.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to hello@newchapteraistudio.com. The operator is New Chapter AI Studio.
