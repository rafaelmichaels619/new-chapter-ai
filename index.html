04 — FAQ

Questions, answered.

Practical notes before a quote. A project is confirmed only in a written agreement.

What is an AI ad?

An AI ad is a film or a set of stills made with generative tools and directed like a production. New Chapter AI Studio uses that process for product ads, spokesperson videos, social clips, brand films, and photography.

How long does a project take?

Turnaround depends on the length, the number of shots, and how many revision rounds the agreement includes. New Chapter AI Studio confirms a schedule in writing before production starts.

How do revisions work?

The written agreement names how many revision rounds are included. Further rounds can be quoted on their own. A revision is a change to a piece already in progress, not a new campaign.

What do you need from me?

A short brief is enough to start: the brand, the product, who the ad is for, the timeline, and any references you already like. Scripts, logos, and product photos help when you have them.

What usage rights do I get?

Usage rights are set in the written agreement for that project. They cover where the finished film or stills may run, and for how long. The agreement is the record, not this page.

How does pricing work?

Every project starts with a custom quote. New Chapter AI Studio does not publish a rate card. Price, schedule, and revision rounds are confirmed in writing before production.

Which formats and aspect ratios can you deliver?

Masters can be delivered for landscape, square, and vertical placements, including 16:9, 1:1, 4:5, and 9:16, in the file types named in the agreement. Social cutdowns for Reels, TikTok, and Shorts can come from the same project.

Who owns the finished content?

The written agreement states who owns the finished film and stills, and what the studio keeps. Portfolio media already on this site is owned by New Chapter AI Studio and is not licensed for reuse by a visit to the site.
