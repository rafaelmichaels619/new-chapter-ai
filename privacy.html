Privacy Policy

Effective November 22, 2026

New Chapter AI Studio LLC, a Wyoming limited liability company (“the studio,” “we”), operates newchapteraistudio.com under the name New Chapter AI Studio. This policy explains what information the site collects and how that information is used.

Who we are

New Chapter AI Studio makes AI-generated films and still images for its portfolio, and produces cinematic ads for brands. This website is a static site. It has no user accounts, no checkout, and no payments.

Information we collect

We collect only what you choose to send. If you email the studio or use the contact form, we receive the details you include, such as your name, email address, brand, the kind of project, and your message.

The site does not set its own cookies, and it does not run advertising trackers. The site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, a privacy-friendly service that uses no cookies and does not track individuals, to count visits.

How we use information

We use what you send to reply, talk about a project, and prepare a quote if you ask for one. We do not sell personal information. We do not share it except when needed to respond to you, to meet a legal duty, or to protect the studio’s rights.

Third-party services

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect standard technical data, such as an IP address and browser details, under GitHub’s own policies.

A film on the site may play from this website or, when a piece is linked that way, from an embedded player on YouTube, Vimeo, or Google Drive. Those hosts may collect data under their own policies when a player loads.

Type on the site is loaded from Google Fonts. Google may receive a request for those font files under its own policies.

Children’s privacy

The site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy. When we do, the effective date at the top of this page will change. The updated policy applies once it is posted here.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@newchapteraistudio.com.
