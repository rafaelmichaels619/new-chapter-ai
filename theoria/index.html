What Theoria is for

Protection, not a playbook.

Theoria helps you spot manipulation and protect yourself. It does not teach you how to manipulate other people.

Stories show a pattern so you can see it, name it, and step out of it. The point is your safety and your judgment, not influence over someone else.

Lessons and stories

A story you can walk through.

Lessons are told as stories. You meet a moment, choose a response, and see what that choice protects. Dilemmas stay open: there is no score for “winning” against another person.

  • Story-driven lessons
  • Dilemmas with more than one honest response
  • A daily insight, included on the free tier

Practice

See the tactic. Keep your ground.

Practice is for recognition. You are not rehearsing how to use a tactic on someone.

Spot the Tactic

Short scenarios. You notice the move in the scene, then choose a response that protects you.

Premium

Pattern Decoder

Take a situation apart and see the pattern in it. Pattern Decoder is part of Premium.

Journal

Write what you noticed. The journal is yours to keep the pattern in view after the story ends.

Your guide

Alexander Graves, and a fox.

Lessons are led by Alexander Graves, a mentor persona in the app. He is a character in the stories, not a therapist and not a person you can hire.

A fox companion stays beside the lesson. It grows as you practice. It does not scold, and it does not disappear if you miss a night.

Companions

One companion, growing with you.

You pick one active companion. On the free tier that is a fox, a puppy, a kitten, or an owl, in a basic home. A free companion’s growth pauses at Young unless you subscribe. Premium companions, their homes, and further accessories stay on Premium. Accessories are earned through practice. They are not sold.

A steady night

Insight, streak, and a skill map.

A daily insight keeps the practice small. A night streak marks the days you return. The skill map shows strengths such as boundaries, empathy, awareness, trust, courage, and self-worth.

  • Daily insight
  • A night streak
  • A skill map
  • A journal for what you want to remember
