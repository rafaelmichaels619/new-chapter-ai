How it works

Check in. Spot it. Step out.

Theoria is for protection. A story shows the pattern so you can name it and keep your ground. It is not a playbook for influencing someone else.

Check in at night

A short reflection, and a story when you want one. Alexander Graves, a persona in Theoria, leads the lessons. He is not a clinician.

Spot the pattern

See the move in the moment. Spot the Tactic is practice for recognition, not for using a tactic on someone.

Protect yourself

Choose a response that keeps you steady. A journal and a daily insight hold what you noticed. A fox companion stays with the practice.
