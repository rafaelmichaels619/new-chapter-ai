Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026

Theoria and this website are operated by New Chapter AI Studio LLC, a Wyoming limited liability company (“we”), under the name New Chapter AI Studio. Theoria is an educational app about recognizing manipulation and protecting yourself. It is not therapy. This policy explains what information the site and the app involve, and how to reach us.

Questions: support@newchapteraistudio.com.

This website

This website is a static site. It does not ask you to create an account. The waitlist form opens an email to support@newchapteraistudio.com with the subject “Theoria waitlist” and the address you typed in the message. Your email app sends that message. We receive the address you send from and anything you write.

The site does not set its own cookies, and it does not run advertising trackers. The site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, a privacy-friendly service that uses no cookies and does not track individuals, to count visits.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect standard technical data, such as an IP address and browser details, under GitHub’s own policies. Type is loaded from Google Fonts. Google may receive a request for those font files under its own policies.

The app is not listed yet

Theoria is not on Google Play yet. Until it is, the only information this project asks for is a waitlist email. The notes below describe the app as it is planned, so you can read them before you install it.

Account data

If you create an account in the app, we receive the email address you use for that account and the progress tied to it. That can include lessons you finish, journal entries you save, the companion you chose, and practice you record. We use that information to run the app for you: to show your place in a lesson, your journal, and your companion.

We do not ask you to send passport numbers, government identity numbers, or payment card numbers by email. Do not put those in a waitlist message or a support email.

Subscriptions and Google Play

Paid plans, when the app is listed, are billed by Google Play. Theoria does not collect or store your card number. Google handles the payment under Google’s terms. We may receive a record that a subscription is active, such as the plan name and whether it is current, so the app can unlock what you paid for. That record is not your card number.

Waitlist email

If you join the waitlist, we use your email to tell you when Theoria is available on Google Play. A waitlist message is not an account and not a purchase. You can ask us to delete it by writing to support@newchapteraistudio.com.

Analytics

On this website, visit counts come from Cloudflare Web Analytics, described above. If the app later uses analytics, this policy will name that service and the effective date will change. We do not sell personal information. We do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

How long we keep information

Waitlist email is kept until we have written to you about availability, or sooner if you ask us to delete it. Account information is kept while your account is open. You may ask us to delete it, except where we must keep a record of a purchase or a legal duty.

Children

Theoria and this website are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email support@newchapteraistudio.com and we will delete it.

United States

This policy is written for people in the United States. If you use the site or the app from elsewhere, you do so on your own initiative.

Changes

We may update this policy. When we do, we will change the effective date at the top. The current policy is the one posted on this page.
